About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight application designed to help you capture your thoughts instantly. It runs smoothly in your browser, ensuring your ideas are always just a click away.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click on create a new note.
  2. Type your ideas into the text area.
  3. Save your note for later review.

Features

  • Lightning fast loading times.
  • Clean and intuitive interface.
  • Secure local browser storage.
    • Offline access to your notes.
    • Easy to organize and search your notes.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N New Note
Ctrl + S Save Note
